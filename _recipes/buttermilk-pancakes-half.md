ingredients:
  Butter:
    - 42.5g unsalted butter
  Dry:
    - 140g plain flour
    - 33g white sugar
    - 5/8 tsp baking powder
    - 1/4 tsp baking soda
    - 1/8 tsp salt
  Wet:
    - 240ml (225g) buttermilk
    - 1 tsp vanilla extract
    - 1 egg

directions:
- Melt the butter and set aside to cool.
- In a medium bowl, whisk together dry ingredients (flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, salt).
- In another medium bowl, whisk together wet ingredients (buttermilk, vanilla extract, egg). You can use the same whisk. Whisk the melted butter into the wet ingredients.
- Pour wet ingredients into dry ingredients and whisk until combined.
- Heat a non-stick pan over medium-low heat.
- Pour 1/3 cup of batter into the pan. Cook until a few bubbles start to reach the top. Gently slide a spatula underneath to flip the pancake and cook the other side until you can see the sides of the pancake look cooked.
- Remove and continue cooking the batter, one pancake at a time until done. The last pancake might be a mega big pancake to use the rest of the batter.

notes:
- The original recipe also adds half a cup of sliced strawberries (folded into the batter before cooking)
- Can put the pancakes in the oven at 93°C to keep warm while cooking the rest.
- Can be stored for 5 days in the fridge, or 3 months in the freezer.
